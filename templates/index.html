Introduction: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae, and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.

📚 What Is StudyAI?

StudyAI is a free, AI-powered study assistant built for students anywhere in the world. Whether you're in middle school, high school, college, or preparing for a professional or competitive exam, StudyAI helps you understand difficult topics, generate structured study notes, and draft blog posts — all in one place, without any cost or account sign-up.

The AI Chat now remembers your conversation, replies in the language you type in, lets you attach files or images and search the live web for up-to-date answers, and suggests smart follow-up questions — so you can go deeper on any topic without retyping context every time. Your chat, notes, and blog history is saved privately in your own browser, never on our servers, and older entries clear themselves automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is StudyAI free to use?

Yes. StudyAI is completely free to use, with no hidden charges or subscription fees.

Can I upload a file or image in the chat?

Yes. Tap the 📎 icon next to the chat box to attach a PDF, Word document, CSV, text/code file, or an image (up to 10MB). StudyAI reads the content and uses it to answer your question.

Can StudyAI search the web for current information?

Yes. Turn on the "🔎 Search the web" toggle above the Ask button, and StudyAI will look up fresh information online and cite its sources in the answer.

Does the AI Chat remember previous messages?

Yes. Within a session, StudyAI keeps track of your conversation so you can ask follow-up questions naturally. Click "New Chat" to start fresh. Past conversations, notes, and blogs are also saved as History right in your browser for up to 30 days.

Where is my history stored?

Entirely on your own device, using your browser's local storage. It is never sent to or saved on our servers. History older than 30 days is removed automatically, and you can clear it anytime from the History panel.

What languages does StudyAI support?

The AI Chat automatically detects and replies in whichever language you type in — English, Hindi, Spanish, French, Arabic, or many others. Notes and Blog tools let you pick a language explicitly, including a free-text "Other" option for anything not in the list.
