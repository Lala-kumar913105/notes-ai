Introduction: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae, and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.

Key Definition: Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Important Points: Takes place in chloroplasts; requires chlorophyll, sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide; releases oxygen as a by-product; occurs mainly in leaves.

Example: A potted plant placed near a sunny window grows faster than one kept in a dark room, demonstrating the role of light in photosynthesis.

Exam Tip: Remember the word equation and the two main stages — the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle (light-independent reactions).
