Hum aapki personal jaankari kisi ko bechte nahi hain. Aapke chat/notes questions AI model providers (jaise OpenRouter aur unke underlying models) ko processing ke liye bheje jaate hain, taaki aapko jawab mil sake. Ye data hum permanently store nahi karte.
